# NavAssist Privacy Policy

NavAssist does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.

- The Accessibility Service is used only to perform system actions you choose in the floating menu
  (Back, Recents, Notifications, Quick settings, Lock screen, Power menu, Screenshot).
  It does not read screen content, keystrokes or passwords.
- The app has no internet permission, no analytics, no ads and no account.
- Settings (chosen buttons, favorite app, opacity) are stored only on your device.
- The installed-app list is read locally to power the Search button and app pickers and never leaves your phone.
