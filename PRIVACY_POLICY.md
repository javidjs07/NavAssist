# NavAssist Privacy Policy

NavAssist has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. The developer does not collect or receive any of your data.

**Accessibility Service.** Used only to perform system actions you choose in the floating menu (Back, Recents,
Notifications, Quick settings, Lock screen, Power menu, Screenshot) and, on phones that ignore the normal Recents
action, a swipe gesture. It does not read screen content, keystrokes or passwords.

**AI assistant (optional).** If you add your own API key, the messages you type or speak are sent directly from your
phone to the AI provider you picked (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or a custom endpoint you entered). Their privacy
policy applies to that data. NavAssist does not send it anywhere else and does not keep chat history after you close
the chat. Your API key is encrypted with the Android Keystore and stored only on your phone.

**Microphone (optional).** Used only while you tap the mic in the AI chat, for speech-to-text through Android's speech
service. Nothing is recorded or stored by NavAssist.

**Screen recording (optional).** Only when you start it. Android asks for your consent each time. Videos are saved on your phone in Movies/NavAssist and never uploaded by NavAssist.

**Photos and videos (optional).** Only if you press Scan on the Cleaning page, Android asks for access so NavAssist can list screenshots and large videos on the screen. File names and sizes are read on your phone and never uploaded. Deleting files always asks for your confirmation.

**Other.** The installed-app list is read locally to power the Search button and app pickers and never leaves your
phone. Settings (chosen buttons, favorite app, opacity, etc.) are stored only on your device.
